Congratulations to Dr. Kaltashov on successfully defending his dissertation!

[Alexander Kaltashov](https://rheoinformatic.com/members/alexander-kaltashov) successfully defended his Ph.D. thesis, titled "Temporal and Spatial Control of Structure and Rheology in Binary Colloidal Gels." This work developed a new theoretical framework for studying the formation and rheology of gels made from two types of colloidal particles, with applications in gradient materials design and 3D printing.

Congratulations, Dr. Kaltashov, on this significant achievement! Wishing you all the best, and we look forward to seeing the impact of your work in the years to come!
